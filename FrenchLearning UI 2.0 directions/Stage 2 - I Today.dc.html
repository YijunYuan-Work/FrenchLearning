Transitions on Today
i.Enter pageHero: opacity 0→1, y 8→0, 240ms ease-out. Rows follow, 50ms stagger, max 3.
ii.Return with new progressCompare dailyProgress to the last count shown (sessionStorage, UI only). Play iii–iv once; otherwise render final state.
iii.Mark completesNumeral: opacity→0, rotate −8°, 160ms. Seal: scale .6→1 + opacity, 240ms, 80ms delay.
iv.Progress updatesSegment scaleX 0→1, 420ms. Odometer translateY one digit, 480ms. "Next up" paper moves to the next row, 200ms.
v.Reduced motionEverything renders in its final state. Odometer swaps the digit. No stagger. Toggle in Tweaks to compare.
