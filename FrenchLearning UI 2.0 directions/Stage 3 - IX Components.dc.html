iv.Motion additions

Row hover / selectBackground to #F3EEE5 / sheet, 180ms. Checkbox fill 140ms. No lift on dictionary rows; fiches lift −2px.
DeleteRows dim to .35 while deleting, then removed; status line replaces the bar.
Modal open / closeScrim fade 200; panel y 12→0 + fade 240 ink. Close 180 ease-in. Mobile sheet: y 100%→0, 280.
Field focusInset ring 1→2px river, instant. Writing lines grow a 1px under-shadow.
Auto-fill resultai-wash appears instantly, fades 600ms; "· auto-filled" note stays until next edit.
Import ticksEach row tick colours on result, 240ms. Counters swap digits without rolling (they change too fast).
Grammar readerSwitching notes: article y 8→0 + fade 240.
Reduced motionAll of the above → instant. Spinners remain (they convey state) but stop rotating; text carries the meaning.
