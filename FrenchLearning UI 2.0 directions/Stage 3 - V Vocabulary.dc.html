Design notes
Dictionary entry, not card. Four columns — margin box · headword · sense · status — on hairline rules. No card chrome at all.
Letter heads appear only for A–Z sort; derived from french with articles (le/la/l'/les) ignored, matching the fr collator.
Selection lives in the margin. The ink bar slides up only when ≥1 is selected, so the page stays quiet.
Confirm replaces window.confirm with a styled alertdialog; same single-flight guard.
"Needs practice / I know this" buttons (when enabled) move into the status column under the meter as two text buttons — not shown here as Vocabulary uses Quiz for confidence.
Mobile & tabletEntry collapses to two rows. Filters move into a sheet; type tabs scroll horizontally. Long-press or the margin box enters selection.
