Il faut que is always followed by the subjunctive, because it expresses necessity rather than a fact.

  1. Take the ils/elles form of the present tense.

Structure
Three numbered parts — I. L'essentiel · II. Grammaire (vocab) / Grammar note · III. Contexte. Vocabulary uses two columns ≥ 880px.
Section select → 4-way tab radio. Changing it invalidates any running auto-fill (unchanged rule).
Auto-fill is a pill beside the French label. Filled fields get a sage wash that fades over 600ms + an "auto-filled" note; fields you touched while it ran are never overwritten (editedFields).
Footer is the single status line: auto-fill progress, errors, saving. Sticky; body scrolls.
While saving the fieldset is disabled + inert, dimmed to 60%; Cancel becomes Close.
Mobile editorFull-screen sheet. Header and footer are sticky; the footer rides on visualViewport above the keyboard. Accent keys become a horizontal strip.
