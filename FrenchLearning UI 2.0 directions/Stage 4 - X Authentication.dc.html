la couverture
i.The cover is a dictionary entry.

The word cahier, typeset as it would appear in the Vocabulary chapter — headword, IPA, gender, definitions, an example. The product introduces itself in its own grammar, with no claims or invented numbers.

ii.The form is the first page.

A bright sheet (#FCFAF6) lies on the paper cover with a soft binding edge. Two numbered tabs — I. Sign in, II. Create account — switch modes without clearing anything typed.

iii.The sommaire is the real table of contents.

Its three groups mirror the sidebar (Pratique · Le cahier · Bureau), with the same numbers and French glosses, so signing in leads straight into a structure you’ve already seen.
